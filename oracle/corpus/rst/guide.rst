Placing an order
================

Choose a *pickup time*, add your items and press ``Order``. You will see a
:ref:`confirmation page <confirmation>` with the total and a link_ to your |receipt|.
Questions? Visit `our help centre <https://example.com/help>`_ or write to support@example.com.

.. note:: Orders placed after 6 PM are baked the next morning.

   Fresh bread is never frozen.

.. warning::
   :class: urgent

   Do not leave the oven door open [1]_.

- Sourdough loaf
- Rye with **caraway** seeds
