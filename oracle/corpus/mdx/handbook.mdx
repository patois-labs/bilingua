# Welcome to {meta.owner}'s bakery

Before your first shift, read this page once and keep it open. It covers the **oven**, the _rota_ and the `till`.
Questions? Ask in the staff channel or email <help@lumen.example>.

## The oven

Press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> + <Kbd>Shift</Kbd> + <Kbd>P</Kbd> to open the oven panel, then pick a program.

<Callout type="warning" title="Hot surfaces">
Never open the door while the fan is running. Wait for the beep.
</Callout>

1. Preheat to 230 degrees.
2. Load the trays (at most {meta.updated - 2020} per rack).
3. Start the timer and note the batch number.

- Sourdough: 35 minutes
- Rye: 45 minutes, [see the recipe](https://example.org/recipes/rye_bread)
- A literal \{ brace and a literal \< bracket stay escaped.
